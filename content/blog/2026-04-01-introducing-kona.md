Meet Kona, your personal habit coach.

Currently, Kona can give suggestions about habits. We're hoping to add more intelligence in the coming months so Kona can turn your daily actions into:

1. Clear reflections on your progress.
2. Smart nudges when you're about to break a streak.
3. Insights that help you focus on what truly matters today.
