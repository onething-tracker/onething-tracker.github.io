ABC's article on looksmaxxing is an eye-opener for us.

Young people use creative ways to define what's good-looking: facial symmetry, jawline definition, and even the angle of the corners of the eyes.

Some even talk about hitting their jaw with a hammer to make it pop more.

We think it's ok to watch those videos for entertainment, but don't take them too seriously.

Going to the gym, cycling, and running are all effective ways to become fitter. They may sound boring, but doing them consistently is hard.

Focus on these and let go of the hammer.
