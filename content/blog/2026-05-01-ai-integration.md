By connecting OneThing to AI clients such as Claude or ChatGPT, we're hoping to inspire more creative ways of habit building. For example,

> "Based on my running record on Garmin, create some habits in OneThing to help me recover quicker."

> "Search some receipts on the internet and create a diet plan on OneThing"

The possibilities are endless.

The key enabler for this will be an MCP, and that's what we'll be building next.
