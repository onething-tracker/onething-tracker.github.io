In April we introduced Kona, your personal habit coach. At the time, Kona could suggest habits and not much else.

We are reimagining the experience of using Kona. We're adding prompt chips to help you communicate with Kona better. They'll range from suggestions for recovering from a broken streak to help with staying motivated and sticking with your habits.
